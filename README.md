# CSCS Test: AI Study Kit

A free kit that turns an AI assistant (Claude, ChatGPT or similar) into a patient tutor for the **CITB Health, Safety and Environment (HS&E) test**, the test most people take before applying for a CSCS card. The tutor teaches from UK health and safety guidance, quizzes you in the real test format and keeps a record of what you know and what you still need to review.

The kit has no exam questions of its own and contains no CITB material. For original practice questions, mock tests and lessons, see the free links at the end.

## What's inside

| File | What it does |
|---|---|
| `CLAUDE.md` | The tutor's instructions. Claude Code reads this file automatically. |
| `TUTOR.md` | The same instructions, for pasting into Claude Projects, a ChatGPT custom GPT or any other assistant. |
| `progress/tracker.md` | A checklist of 21 topics, grouped under the test's five areas, where the tutor records what you know. |
| `sessions/SESSION-TEMPLATE.md` | A short log the tutor fills in after each study session. |
| `prompts.md` | Ready-to-use things to say to your tutor. |

## How to use it

**With Claude Code (recommended)**
1. Download or clone this folder.
2. Open it in Claude Code and say: *"Let's start. What should I study first?"*
3. After each session the tutor updates `progress/tracker.md` and writes a short log in `sessions/`.

**With Claude Projects, ChatGPT or another assistant**
1. Create a project or custom assistant.
2. Paste the whole of `TUTOR.md` into its instructions.
3. Keep `progress/tracker.md` up to date yourself, or paste it in at the start of each session.

## The test, in brief
- **Format (operatives test):** 50 multiple-choice questions in 45 minutes.
- **To pass:** 45 correct answers (90%).
- **Fee:** £23.50 per test.
- **If you fail:** you can book again, after a short wait set by CITB's terms.
- **Languages:** voiceover is available in 14 languages.
- **For your card:** CSCS asks for a test pass from the last two years. The card itself costs £36, rising to £42 from 1 February 2027.
- **Other levels:** supervisors and managers take their own HS&E tests, in the same 50-question format.

Facts from CITB and CSCS, checked November 2026. Rules can change, so always check CITB before you book and CSCS before you apply for a card.

## A word of care
An AI tutor can be wrong. The tutor is told to follow official guidance and to say when it is unsure, but CITB, CSCS and the Health and Safety Executive are always the final word. If an answer looks odd, check it.
