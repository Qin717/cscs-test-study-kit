# Things to Say to Your Tutor

- "Let's start. What should I study first?"
- "What is due for review today?"
- "Teach me working at height, one piece at a time."
- "Quiz me on safety signs: colours and shapes."
- "Give me 10 questions on manual handling, with the answer after each one."
- "Give me site situations and ask me what I should do."
- "I keep mixing up these duties. Help me tell them apart."
- "Give me a full 50-question mock test and mark it at the end."
- "My test is on [date]. Make me a study plan."
- "Show me my progress."
- "It's the night before my test. What should I look at?"
